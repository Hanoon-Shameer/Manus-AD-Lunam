### PROJECT: MANUS AD LUNAM

This project is an model based on an idea made by ABHAY VINOD and to be produced by **ABHAY VINOD** AND **HANOON SHAMEER** with their team which will be facilitated by the **IT Club Officials**.It is based upon a space rover built for the moon which is controlled by AI to understand hand gestures used for its movement and functions to traverse the terrain and transmit data between a control center and the rover itself with robotic arms to hold onto lunar rocks and to be given for testing to the control center present right next to the simulated/hand-built terrain. This project has been given a separate room (generously given by the IT Club) to be exhibited and requires the utmost functionality and appeal within the given time frame and space. The project requires **2-3 programmers (not including the lead)** and a team of people to design the rover itself and its accessories.
